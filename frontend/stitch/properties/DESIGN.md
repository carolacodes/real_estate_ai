# design.md

## Project
AURA Prime Real Estate — Modern & Luxury Editorial Real Estate Platform with Integrated AI Advisor

## Visual Direction & Brand Identity
- Editorial, high-end, architectural luxury inspired by Homelytics & Alveo
- Clean neutral palette with warm off-whites, architectural deep obsidian/charcoal, champagne warm beige accents, subtle borders, and delicate glassmorphism
- High visual whitespace rhythm, balanced typographic hierarchy, and generous container paddings

## Typography Scale
- Font Family Headings: "Plus Jakarta Sans", -apple-system, sans-serif, with letter-spacing -0.03em
- Font Family Body: "Inter", -apple-system, sans-serif
- Hero Headline: 4rem - 5.5rem, weight 600/700, leading 1.05
- Section Heading: 2.5rem - 3.25rem, weight 600, leading 1.15
- Card Title: 1.25rem, weight 600

## Components
- Floating AI Assistant with glassmorphic modal, prompt chips, typing state, property preview cards inside stream
- Hero Alveo-inspired: expansive full-width luxury composition, architectural imagery, floating badges, inline high-performance search engine
- Luxury Property Cards: minimal aspect-ratio 4:3 / 16:10, pill tags, clean spec badges (bed, bath, sqft), subtle hover zoom
- Search & Filter bar: integrated segmented filter with Location, Type, Price Range, and instant search CTA
