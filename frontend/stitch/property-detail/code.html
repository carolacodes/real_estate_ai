Architectural Monograph

Radical lightness framed in Board-Formed Concrete & Low-Iron Glass

Anchored to an exclusive promontory on upper Bellagio Road, The Glass Pavilion & Sanctuary represents an uncompromising dialogue between organic topography and heroic West Coast modernism. Designed by Olson Kundig Associates, the primary volume floats sixteen feet above the native canyon floor, capturing unbroken panoramas across Stone Canyon Reservoir toward the Pacific horizon.

The structural diagram pairs monolithic board-formed concrete mass walls with ultra-slender blackened steel mullions. Low-iron solar-selective glazing ensures total chromatic neutrality indoors while delivering triple-glazed thermal isolation. Floor plates transition continuously from brushed grey limestone into cantilevered ipe decks with invisible recessed thresholds.
